## One-sentence thesis

> Foundations of Consciousness Psychology proposes that personality develops
> toward God-conscious integration through meaningful choice, faith under
> uncertainty, response to infinite love, soul growth, and service.

## Methodological stance

Lead with the **gaps in contemporary psychology** (personhood, meaning,
transcendence), use grounded-theory inquiry, and present the Urantia-derived
model as **one comparative framework** — not as the opening premise. Every claim
is labelled along the chain:

```
TEXTUAL  ->  INTERPRETIVE  ->  HYPOTHESIS  ->  EMPIRICAL TESTING
```

## Two flagship hypotheses

1. **The Agondonter Hypothesis** — individuals who develop stable *faith under
   uncertainty* display distinctive psychological characteristics distinct from
   both conventional religiosity and secular resilience.
2. **The Infinite Love Hypothesis** — a stable perception of unconditional divine
   love ("Ultimate Attachment") produces measurable developmental effects.
