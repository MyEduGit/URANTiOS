# Construct Matrix - Consciousness Psychology

Project: **The Foundations of Consciousness Psychology: The Mathematics of Infinite Love and the Agondonter Personality**

Status: v0.1 construct bridge. This document translates the project from source categories into academic constructs and testable hypotheses.

> Provenance: imported verbatim from Google Drive
> (`Construct-Matrix-Consciousness-Psychology.md`, 2026-06-22).

Method rule:

```text
TEXTUAL -> INTERPRETIVE -> HYPOTHESIS -> EMPIRICAL TESTING
```

Antigravity benchmark challenge: a psychology department will not accept a construct as empirical psychology merely because the Urantia Book says something. The construct must become operationally defined, measurable, testable, and reproducible.

Methodological pivot: this matrix is now downstream of the grounded theory design. Its constructs should be introduced after grounded-theory categories reveal gaps such as meaning, purpose, spirituality, resilience, uncertainty, integration, faith, or transcendence. The Urantia-derived framework is a comparative candidate, not the opening premise.

## Priority Constructs for a Defensible PhD Proposal

1. **Faith Under Uncertainty**
   Strongest empirical bridge. Connects UB Paper 3 and Paper 50 to uncertainty tolerance, resilience, meaning, and religious coping.

2. **Ultimate Attachment**
   Strongest psychology-of-religion bridge. Connects perceived divine love to Attachment-to-God literature.

3. **Agondonter Development**
   Original contribution. Must be built from Faith Under Uncertainty plus measurable resilience/meaning outcomes.

4. **God-Consciousness Psychology**
   Field-level interpretive frame. Needs glossary and literature map before it can become a defensible academic category.

## Operationalization Drafts

### Faith Under Uncertainty

Working definition:

```text
The capacity to sustain value-directed trust, meaning, loyalty, and service when evidence, certainty, or visible confirmation is delayed or absent.
```

Candidate dimensions:

- Trust under incomplete knowledge
- Meaning stability under uncertainty
- Loyalty under delayed proof
- Service orientation under adversity
- Reduced existential destabilization
- Perseverance without material confirmation

### Ultimate Attachment

Working definition:

```text
The experienced perception of God as unconditionally loving, relationally available, and developmentally stabilizing.
```

Candidate dimensions:

- Secure divine relationship
- Low anxiety about divine abandonment
- Low avoidance of intimacy with God
- Perceived unconditional love
- Shame resilience
- Safe-haven function in distress
- Secure-base function for moral growth

### Agondonter Development

Working definition:

```text
The development of resilient, service-oriented, meaning-stable personality through faith under uncertainty, isolation, delayed proof, and adversity.
```

Candidate dimensions:

- Faith without sight
- Perseverance in isolation
- Adversity tolerance
- Meaning stability
- Purpose stability
- Service orientation
- Spiritual courage
