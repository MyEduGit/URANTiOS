## 1. Why Adventism matters to this project

Revelation 14:12 — "Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that
keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (KJV) — is the only NT
occurrence of the exact English phrase "the faith of Jesus," and
Seventh-day Adventism is the one major tradition that made the verse its
identity text. The denomination has therefore argued internally, for 175
years, over precisely this project's question: is "the faith of Jesus" faith
IN him, the faith TAUGHT by him, his OWN faith given to us, or the same KIND
of faith he had? Every logical option in `03_TERMINOLOGY_AND_ONTOLOGY.md`
has an Adventist advocate.

## 2. Chronological findings

### 2.1 Sabbatarian origins (1846–1850): the identity text

- Joseph Bates, *The Seventh Day Sabbath, A Perpetual Sign* (2nd ed., 1847),
  deploys Rev 14:12 (KJV) as the mark of the end-time people; Adventist
  historiography (ESDA; Andrews University) records Bates uniting the little
  flock "on the commandments of God and faith or testimony of Jesus" (V1).
  Full text: Project Gutenberg #27266 (located, unfetched).
- James White (1850, *The Present Truth*): the operative pioneer reading is
  **fides quae** — "as these, and every other requirement peculiar to the
  gospel, are all embraced in the faith of Jesus, it is evident that the
  commandments of God are not the sayings of Christ and his apostles" (V1) —
  i.e., "faith of Jesus" = the gospel requirements Jesus taught, paired with
  the Decalogue. The 1849 hymnbook title *Hymns for God's Peculiar People That
  Keep the Commandments of God and the Faith of Jesus* (V1) shows the verse
  functioning as the movement's self-designation.

### 2.2 Ellen G. White and 1888: the soteriological redefinition

- The pivotal statement, Ms 24, 1888 (= Selected Messages bk 3, 168–172; the
  1888 Materials), **V2** as a composite whose exact sentence order requires
  primary confirmation:
  > "The faith of Jesus. It is talked of, but not understood... Jesus becoming
  > our sin-bearer that He might become our sin-pardoning Saviour... He was
  > treated as we deserve to be treated... Faith in the ability of Christ to
  > save us amply and fully and entirely is the faith of Jesus."
- Same context (V1): "The commandments of God have been proclaimed, but the
  faith of Jesus has not been proclaimed by Seventh-day Adventists as of equal
  importance, the law and the gospel going hand in hand."
- Review and Herald, April 1, 1890 (= 1SM 372), **V2**: justification by faith
  "is the third angel's message in verity."
- THEOLOGICAL CLAIM (White's own reading): "the faith of Jesus" is faith **in**
  Jesus' substitutionary sin-bearing — an objective reading welded to the law
  so that "law and gospel go hand in hand." In the retrieved material she does
  **not** define it as Jesus' own subjective faith.

### 2.3 Waggoner and Jones: the subjective turn

- E. J. Waggoner, *The Glad Tidings* (1900) on Gal 2:16, **V2** — the clearest
  early Adventist subjective-genitive reading anywhere:
  > "...believing in Christ, we are justified by the faith of Christ, since we
  > have Him personally dwelling in us, exercising His own faith."
  And (V1) he connects this expressly to Rev 14:12 — "the faith of Christ,"
  "just as in Rev. 14:12 we have 'the faith of Jesus,'" with Jesus as "the
  Author and Finisher of faith."
  **This is the strongest historical antecedent of the project's "believing
  WITH Jesus" thesis inside Christianity's own literature** — justifying faith
  as Christ's own faith exercised by the indwelling Christ.
- A. T. Jones (1893/1895 General Conference Bulletins; *Lessons on Faith*):
  PARAPHRASE — commandments and the faith of Jesus inseparable; "the faith of
  Jesus Christ alone saves the soul — at the beginning, at the end, and all the
  way between." The widely attributed Jones definition (Rev 14:12 = Jesus' own
  faith which he gives believers) is **UNVERIFIED verbatim** this session;
  check the 1895 GCB sermons "on the faith of Jesus."
- FACT (historiography): Jones and Waggoner presented righteousness by faith at
  the 1888 Minneapolis General Conference against contested reception; Wieland
  & Short's *1888 Re-Examined* (1950/1987) founded the 1888 Message Study
  Committee stream, which perpetuates the Jones–Waggoner subjective reading —
  classified here per spec as a **partisan stream**, balanced by denominational
  responses (George Knight's historical work; official critiques).

### 2.4 Commentary-era and modern scholarship

- SDA Bible Commentary vol. 7 (1957) ad loc. Rev 14:12: located, **UNVERIFIED
  wording — not cited**.
- Ángel Manuel Rodríguez (BRI), "Keeping the Faith of Jesus" (Adventist
  Review): PARAPHRASE — the Greek is ambiguous; options: (a) Jesus' teachings,
  (b) Jesus' faithfulness (subjective), (c) the believer's saving faith in
  Christ (objective); he concludes contextually for (c).
- Ekkehardt Mueller (BRI), "The End Time Remnant in Revelation," JATS 11/1
  (2000): PARAPHRASE — Rev 12:17, 13:10, 14:12 as interlocking remnant
  descriptions (perseverance/faithfulness frame).
- Herbert E. Douglass, *The Faith of Jesus* (1991) and the harvest/Last
  Generation Theology stream: PARAPHRASE — the faith of Jesus = the same faith
  Jesus himself had, reproducible in a final generation that vindicates God.
  The **exemplary** reading, at maximum strength.
- Morris Venden (*95 Theses on Righteousness by Faith*, 1987; *The Obedience
  of Faith*): V1 thesis samples ("Righteousness equals Jesus...") — his
  specific of/in treatment UNVERIFIED.
- Jack Sequeira (*Beyond Belief*): PARAPHRASE — objective universal gospel
  ("Christ actually obtained salvation, full and complete, for the entire
  human race") vs. subjective appropriation; warns against collapsing gospel
  into experience.
- Critical external voice: Answering Adventism, "Faith OF Jesus vs. Faith IN
  Jesus?" — PARAPHRASE — frames the SDA stake in the KJV rendering within the
  wider genitive debate.
- Sigve Tonstad — the bridge figure between this corpus and the academic
  pistis Christou debate (his Revelation scholarship and published
  faith-of-Christ work): treated in `06_PISTIS_CHRISTOU_DEBATE.md` and
  `10_HISTORICAL_THEOLOGY.md`; his Adventist location noted here per spec.

## 3. The distinction map (research question 7, Adventist column)

| Reading of "the faith of Jesus" (Rev 14:12) | Meaning | Adventist advocates |
|---|---|---|
| A. Fides quae — faith TAUGHT by Jesus | gospel requirements as content | James White (1850, V1); pioneer usage; option (a) in Rodríguez |
| B. Objective — faith IN Jesus | trust in his sin-bearing, "ability... to save us amply and fully" | Ellen White Ms 24, 1888 (V2); mainstream SDA; Rodríguez's conclusion |
| C. Subjective — Christ's OWN faith given to the believer | the indwelling Christ "exercising His own faith" | Waggoner (V2); Jones (attributed, unverified); 1888MSC stream |
| D. Exemplary — the same KIND of faith Jesus had | Jesus' human faith as reproducible model | Douglass; Last Generation Theology |
| E. Faithfulness — Jesus' covenant fidelity | perseverance frame (12:17; 13:10; 14:12) | option (b) in Rodríguez; Mueller |

**Preserved disagreements (Standard 7):** (1) Ellen White's B is not
Waggoner's C, though both were "1888 voices" — the 1888MSC tradition tends to
merge them; (2) Douglass's D vs. Sequeira's objective-gospel priority is a
live intra-Adventist fault line; (3) official BRI scholarship leans B against
both the KJV-preaching tradition A and LGT's D; (4) James White's pioneer A
differs from all later devotional readings and is rarely acknowledged.

## 4. Relation to the project thesis (INFERENCE)

The Adventist archive independently generated, inside a Bible-only
hermeneutic, the full ladder of `03_TERMINOLOGY_AND_ONTOLOGY.md`: from faith
taught by Jesus (A), through faith in Jesus (B), to the faith OF Jesus as his
own communicable trust (C) and as reproducible pattern (D). Waggoner's C is
materially close to Urantia 196:0.13's "believe *with* him" and to the
appropriation language of 101:6.17 — a convergence achieved without any
contact with the Urantia text (which postdates him). This strengthens the
case that the faith-OF-Jesus reading is a recurrent, text-driven recovery,
not an idiosyncrasy of any one tradition. (Conversely, per Standard 9,
nothing here validates the Urantia account historically; the convergence is a
phenomenon to note, not a proof.)
