### Revelation 14:12
KJV "the commandments of God, and **the faith of Jesus**" (V) · NKJV same
(V-partial) · NRSV/NRSVue "hold fast to **the faith of Jesus**" (R) · BSB
"the faith of Jesus" (V) · ESV "**their faith in** Jesus" (V) · NASB95
"their faith in Jesus" (V) · NET "hold to their faith in Jesus" (V) · NIV
"remain **faithful to** Jesus" (V — a loyalty rendering) · GNT "are faithful
to Jesus" (V) · **Cornilescu: "și credința lui Isus" (V via Adventist
workstream)** — the Romanian genitive preserves the ambiguity exactly as KJV.

### Hebrews 12:2
Greek has no possessive. KJV/NKJV/ESV/NRSV supply "**our** faith" (V/R);
**NIV 2011 deliberately renders "the pioneer and perfecter of faith"** (V).
τελειωτής is a NT hapax (V); ἀρχηγός = pioneer/founder/champion (V; cf. Heb
2:10 ἀρχηγὸν τῆς σωτηρίας, R). O'Collins & Kendall (TS 53, 1992) flag the
unwarranted "our" as a symptom of the tradition's unasked question (see 10).

## 3. NET Bible translator-note evidence

V-partial (netbible.org blocked; fragments via secondary pages): the NET note
at Rom 3:22 states that "an increasing number of NT scholars are arguing" for
the subjective genitive ("Christ's faith" or "Christ's faithfulness"), adding
that on this reading "the object of faith is a worthy object, for he himself
is faithful." FACT (V pattern): NET renders subjectively at Rom 3:22, Gal
2:16 (×2), Gal 3:22, Phil 3:9 (×2), Eph 3:12 — but objectively at Rev 14:12.

## 5. Assessment (no pretended certainty)

TEXTUAL OBSERVATION: every verified Greek construction is formally ambiguous;
the data (anarthrous Pauline formulae; articular τῇ in Gal 2:20; articular τὴν
πίστιν Ἰησοῦ in Rev 14:12; pronoun-genitive Eph 3:12; pronoun-less Heb 12:2)
underdetermine the choice. The translation record now spans all answers: NET
subjective, NRSVue "faith of," NIV/ESV/NASB/NKJV objective, NIV-at-Rev-14:12
loyalty. INFERENCE (with Easter 2010, see 06): grammar alone will not settle
it; construals of Paul's gospel decide. The project's larger case therefore
rests on the wider corpus (04, 07, 09, 10), not on winning the genitive.
