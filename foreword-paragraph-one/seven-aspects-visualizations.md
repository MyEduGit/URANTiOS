### Consciousness Tier Map

```
SUPERCONSCIOUS ─── 7. Revelatory Reception
                       │

                       │

                       │
UNCONSCIOUS ───── 4. Conceptual Poverty
```

### Why Seven?

The Urantia Book's cosmology is pervaded by septenary structure: seven
superuniverses, seven adjutant mind-spirits, seven psychic circles,
seven mansion worlds. That this opening paragraph yields exactly seven
aspects of mortal cognition is structurally consistent with the book's
architecture. The seven also echo the theosophical "Seven Principles
of Man" (Sthula Sharira → Atma) and the Kabbalistic seven lower
Sephiroth — universal patterns of consciousness stratification.
