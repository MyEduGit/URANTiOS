# Vocabulary Bank

Target lexis for the twelve handouts, drawn from the Census Household Form 2026. Level tags are the level at which the item is *first taught*; items recur later as recycled language.

Items marked ⚠ sit in a sensitive block (rule R3) — teach them receptively, in the third person, without personal disclosure tasks.
