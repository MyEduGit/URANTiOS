# Filling In the Form — English for the 2026 Census

An ESL handout series built from one authentic document: the Australian Bureau of Statistics **Census Household Form**, for Census night **Tuesday 11 August 2026**.

Twelve handouts, four modules, pre-A1 to B1. Designed for AMEP, TAFE, community college and volunteer tutoring in Australia.

## Two rules to read before teaching any of it

**Practise on a fictional household, never a real one.** No handout collects a learner's real name, address, income, health condition, visa status or religion. Learners fill in a personal copy they keep and take home; nothing personal is handed in, displayed or marked.

**Teach the form; do not complete the form.** When a learner asks what to put for their own situation, the answer is the ABS help line **1800 181 227** or TIS National **131 450** — not the teacher's guess.
